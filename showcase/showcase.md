# Facet

Seven stones. One beautiful chain reaction. Facet is a gem-matching puzzle set on a jewel-lit lapidary bench. Take hold of a stone, carry it onto its neighbor, and release: a line of three or more shatters, making room for the next possibility.

Every clear leaves its mark. Stones beside it crack under strain until they become flawed — poised to shatter with any clear that touches them, and worth double when they do. Build pressure in a corner, then spend it in a reaction that travels across the board.

Longer lines reward a finer cut. Four stones leave a brilliant that takes its surrounding ring. Five make a multicolored prism: swap it to take every stone of one kind. Cross two lines to earn a star that sweeps its entire row and column.

As fresh stones fall, a single swap can grow into a climbing chain of glass, light, and rising notes. Reach the target to open the next level. There is no ticking clock: study the field, find the flaw, and see how far one thoughtful move can go.
